# Approved logo / use guide

The supplied four lockups reuse the exact approved paths and established transforms from the Musical Window package. The lowercase handwritten/guitar-tail exploration and rejected guitar-outline logo are not used. Do not replace PARA with font text.

## Choosing a version

| Condition | Use |
|---|---|
| Expressive logo on Ivory | `Grain_Gradient/para-horizontal-light-grain.svg` |
| Expressive logo on Espresso | `Grain_Gradient/para-horizontal-dark-grain.svg` |
| Compact navigation / small reproduction | `Solid/para-horizontal-espresso.svg` or Ivory on dark |
| Narrow composition | Established stacked lockup |
| Existing brand context / app identity | Symbol alone, with appropriate clear space |
| Editorial signature | Wordmark alone; never treated as a substitute for the symbol in favicon use |
| Printing / low-resolution output | Black or white solid vector |

The `Web` subfolders crop the viewBox to drawing bounds without changing any path or transform. Add clear space in the layout. High-resolution transparent PNGs are in `PNG`; SVG is preferred where supported.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- Horizontal: 75 source units around the logo, equal to one quarter of the 300-unit symbol height. Grain preferred at ≥280 px total width; solid minimum 140 px.
- Stacked: one quarter of the symbol’s height on all sides; grain preferred ≥200 px wide, solid ≥100 px.
- Symbol: 25% of symbol height as clear space; grain ≥72 px, solid ≥32 px. Small favicon proposals are separate adaptations, not the master.
- Wordmark: 25% of cap height as clear space; grain ≥180 px wide, solid ≥100 px.

These are tested practical starting points, not permission to ignore a difficult background. Increase size or use a solid version if thin ties/counters soften in the actual rendering.

## Alignment and placement

Align the horizontal logo optically with navigation text; the symbol is intentionally taller than the PARA capitals. Preserve their internal relationship. Use a quiet footprint in navigation and a more generous clear-space area in a footer. Never add a handwritten “Music” descriptor to these masters.

Use the dark treatment on Espresso and the light treatment on Ivory. Do not place grain logos across a busy photograph or a gradient with similar brightness. A solid logo on a quiet reserved area is preferable in those conditions.

## Incorrect use

Do not stretch, shear, re-space, mirror, crop the actual symbol, fill the counters, rearrange the musical blocks, add a shadow/glow, introduce metallic highlights or change colours outside the token system. Do not substitute supporting artwork for the official symbol.

## Proposals

`App_Favicon_Proposals` contains two padded app tiles using the original symbol, a 32 px original-symbol favicon and a 16 px simplified proposal with the two thin ties removed. The 16 px form changes geometry deliberately and is not approved as a new master. ICO and PNG exports are provided. Review these adaptations before shipping them.
