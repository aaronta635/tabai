# Real photography — landing page only

No photograph was supplied or processed in this package. Use your own pictures of Henry, Aaron and genuine practice/performance moments. Photography belongs on the landing page; keep the app and resource illustrations in the approved drawn language.

## Choose and crop

- Practice: hands finding a chord, a teacher listening, a pencil on a score, quiet concentration. Prefer moments with a clear action rather than posed thumbs-up portraits.
- Performance: one musician, the instrument and the atmosphere of the room. Preserve the feeling of a real stage; do not imply an endorsement or fabricate an audience.
- Leave breathing space around faces, fingers and the instrument’s neck. Use 3:2 or 4:3 crops for scenes, 4:5 for portraits. Prepare mobile crops separately; do not simply centre-crop every desktop photo.
- Use the photo as a substantial focal point. Avoid placing detailed artwork and a detailed photo at equal size in the same small area. One photograph plus a restrained icon or caption is often enough.

## Warm indoor practice

Correct exposure and white balance first. Preserve natural skin tones and timber detail. Keep warmth in midtones rather than applying a heavy brown/sepia filter. An optional Sand wash at 3–6% opacity can relate the image to the palette, but omit it if skin or whites turn yellow.

## Dark performance photographs

Retain believable stage light, including colour already in the photo. Protect highlights on faces and instruments; do not lift dark areas until sensor noise becomes distracting. **Do not add grain to already noisy stage photographs.** The illustration texture does not need to be imposed on every image.

## Supplied devices

- `para-photo-lower-shade.svg`: transparent Espresso gradient, ending at 42% opacity. Adjust or omit; it is not proof that overlaid text meets contrast requirements. Test text against the final crop.
- `para-photo-rising-frame-ivory.svg` / `...-espresso.svg`: substantial frame with a gently rising, transparent aperture. Overlay it above an actual image; no image is embedded. The frame is optional, not a mandatory border on every photo.
- If a very clean photograph needs a slight tactile relationship, use the separate grain tile at only 2–5% total opacity, and inspect faces at 100%. Grain is never a substitute for a well-exposed image.

Keep a clean original and non-destructive edit layers. Make image permission, consent and final crop decisions when the real photos are selected. No photo permissions or crop approvals are assumed by this package.
